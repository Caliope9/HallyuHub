# HallyuHub Image Policy

HallyuHub must not use artist, idol, agency, fansite, press, social media, Google, Pinterest, Instagram, TikTok, YouTube thumbnail, or news images unless the project has a clear legal right to use them.

Accepted image sources for production:

- Images uploaded by users who confirm they own the content or have permission.
- Official press kits only when usage permission is explicit.
- Wikimedia Commons or other Creative Commons media only when the license allows the intended app use and attribution is stored.
- Public domain media with source evidence.
- Original HallyuHub-created placeholders, gradients, avatars, banners, or illustrations.

Every reusable image record should preserve:

- `imageUrl`
- `imageSource`
- `imageLicense`
- `attribution`
- `author`
- `licenseUrl`
- `officialUrl` when relevant

Biographies must be written in original wording. Do not copy official profiles, wiki pages, agency text, fan translations, news articles, or social captions literally.

For App Store and Play Store review, keep placeholders until each production image has verifiable rights, attribution, and a moderation path.
